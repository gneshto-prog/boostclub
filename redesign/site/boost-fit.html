FAQ

Before you come.

What do I need?
Trainers, sportswear, water. A jacket for the walk to the club.
What if it rains?
Light rain, we train. Heavy rain, we announce in the group by 07:15 what we do.
I'm not in shape, can I come?
Yes. Every exercise has an easy version. Tell Gabriel at the start.
Do I have to be a Boost Club client?
No. Anyone can come.
Can I bring kids or my dog?
Kids from 14, with you. Dogs in the park yes, in the club no.
Do you film?
Yes, we film for Instagram. If you don't want to be in it, tell us at the start.
What does it cost?
The training is free. The entrance is the recovery nutrition at the club: 35 lei (protein shake, electrolyte drink, tea). 10-entrance card: 290 lei, valid at Boost Fit and at Boost Club.
Do I need to tell you I'm coming?
Yes, reply in the WhatsApp group so we know how many we are.
