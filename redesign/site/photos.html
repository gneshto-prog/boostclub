Generator bake-off

Same brief, four generators

"A member receives a berry shake at the counter", with your two photos as the room and light reference.

  1. Use

    Nano Banana 2.1 for people

    Natural skin and expressions. Its weak spot is the wall sign, which can come out blank or bent.

  2. Use

    FLUX 3 when the room must be exact

    It reproduces the sign and even the results wall. Slightly more catalogue-looking.

  3. Avoid

    GPT Image (earlier rounds) and Soul 2

    GPT Image gives the glossy ad look you called AI-like. Soul 2 redrew the handshake with a wrong logo.

  4. Best

    Your real photos, extended and upscaled

    Editing your originals only warmed them slightly, so there is nothing to gain there. Outpaint plus upscale on real photos beats anything generated.
