Frequently asked questions.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The body assessment and first consultation are free.

How long does the first visit take?

About 25–30 minutes: the body composition scan, results explained, and a discussion about your goals.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

Gabriel messages you on WhatsApp to agree on a time. Your visit is confirmed once he sends you the day, time and address. If you haven't heard from us, message us on WhatsApp.

What happens after the free visit? You leave with your results and explanations. If you want ongoing support, we show you the options, what they include and what they cost.
