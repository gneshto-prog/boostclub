## Recommended shooting style

- Prefer documentary moments over posed team photographs.
- Keep the wider club visible whenever possible.
- Use soft morning daylight and the real Boost Club environment.
- Include hands, tables, cups, plants, notes and small details that make the space feel lived-in.
- Photograph a mix of wide scenes, medium interactions and close details.
- Avoid harsh gym lighting, clinical compositions, product-first images and empty stock-photo smiles.
